# Atomic-research reference sources

These sheets are design-intent references, not engineering or radiation-safety plans.

## Climatron-derived glazing

### Verified from official archival source

- Library of Congress, Historic American Buildings Survey **HABS MO-1135-L**:
  [Missouri Botanical Garden, Climatron](https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/mo0413/).
- HABS photograph 6 shows the structure’s large tubular compression members,
  circular nodes, slender tension members, and separate suspended glazing layer.
- HABS photograph 7 provides a closer view of the tension/suspension system.

### Verified from published building history

- Completed in 1960; an unpartitioned geodesic half-sphere approximately 42 m in
  diameter and 21 m high.
- Aluminium tubes work in compression and aluminium rods in tension.
- Original enclosure used rigid acrylic/Plexiglas panels. Renovation replaced the
  deteriorated acrylic with laminated, heat-strengthened glass.

### VERDANT interpretation

- Eden Prime uses a Climatron-derived aluminium space frame with hexagonal pane
  modules, per the project’s settled art direction. It is not a literal Climatron
  reconstruction.
- Original acrylic and later laminated-glass repair panes coexist to expose two
  maintenance eras.
- Dimensions and geodesic frequency remain governed by Mark and Claude’s HABS
  measured drawings, not the concept sheet.

## Gamma garden

### Verified historical method

- Atomic gardening was mutation breeding using ionizing radiation, commonly a
  cobalt-60 gamma source.
- Large gamma gardens were often circular/radial fields of roughly five acres,
  divided into crop wedges around a retractable central source.
- Dose fell with distance: plants nearest the source commonly died; the next band
  showed severe growth abnormalities; farther out was a viable high-mutation band;
  near the edge plants approached the control condition.
- The source was lowered into below-grade shielding before staff entered.
- IAEA overview: [What is Mutation Breeding?](https://www.iaea.org/newscenter/news/what-is-mutation-breeding).
- Historical visual anchor: Brookhaven gamma-field crop photograph, circa 1957,
  preserved on Wikimedia Commons as `Plants in gamma field at Brookhaven`.

### VERDANT interpretation

- The lethal, deformation, selection, and control bands are exaggerated just enough
  to read from a level overlook while preserving the historical radial logic.
- Tags, stakes, sample removal, interlocks, warning lamps, and a source-control hut
  make the field read as an actively managed research instrument rather than a
  mystical garden.
